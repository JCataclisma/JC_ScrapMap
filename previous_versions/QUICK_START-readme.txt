If you don't wanna the full info from README (which got quite important stuff inside), this one might do the hot start for you:

-download/unpack the app files - let the folder ANYWHERE you want, as long as it has both reading AND writing privileges;

-open Steam to speed things up and let it open - do NOT open the game;

-execute the file " Start JC ScrapMap.cmd "  - windows will ask for special authorization for that;

-click the option 2: "Generate" - the game will automatically start, then you must let our app running in the background throughout the whole time (it might auto-close when finished);

-load your savegame (which might get a little slower, as the roads/biomes exporter does silently run in this stage) and, once open, wait or play a little bit, something around 15 seconds or more, just to make sure our "exporter" has enough time to do its thing;

-exit the savegame;

-close Scrap Mechanic;

-now you should receive a message either for SUCCESS or FAIL/ERROR, while our app has created a log/report called " JC_ScrapMap_Diagnostic.txt " ;

=======================================
IMPORTANT: you will NOT need to do this process above "never again" for this savegame, unless you notice loss of data due to some game update or something like that.
=======================================

Now that your map data is generated, you can either click already option 1 OPEN MAP, or close the app and execute it again.
EVERY TIME, from now on, that you click "Opem Map", the app will silently retrieve your player's position, as well as new data regarding physical beacons you might have deployed or removed.
